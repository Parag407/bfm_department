About the Program

Empowering the Next Generation of Investors

The BFM program integrates theoretical learning with real-world financial applications. We espouse the salient points of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, focusing on skill development, financial literacy, and holistic growth.

  • Collaborative Certifications

    Joint programs with NISM and ANMI focusing on essential financial concepts and practices.

  • Practical Exposure

    Regular industry field visits to BSE, NSE, and the RBI Monetary Museum.

  • Industry Interaction

    Guest lectures on CFP, Business Valuation, Actuarial Science, and Derivatives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the BFM programme at Hinduja College.

Candidates aspiring to pursue B.Com (Financial Markets) Degree shall have passed XII std. examination of the Maharashtra State Board of Higher Secondary Education or its equivalent.

General Category

45% Aggregate

Reserved Category

40% Aggregate

Admission is strictly based on merit. No entrance exam is required for this programme.

Learners will develop a solid understanding of role, structure, and functioning of financial markets.

  • Develop analytical reasoning to assess investment opportunities and manage risks.
  • Analyse global challenges and exposure to newer avenues in finance.
  • Build competencies in professional skills and ethics required for financial growth.
  • Gain research proficiency by evaluating financial viability of various projects.

The evaluation is divided into two comprehensive parts to ensure balanced academic assessment:

40%
Continuous Internal Assessment

Projects, assignments, and class participation.

60%
Semester End Examination

Final comprehensive written assessments.
